# Input sources

The MIT license covers original CGP-Align software. Third-party data, pretrained models and rule libraries retain their source terms.

- Cell Painting profiles and molecular metadata: JUMP/cpg0016, https://github.com/broadinstitute/cellpainting-gallery (CC0).
- Compound-gene annotations: MotiVE, https://github.com/carpenter-singh-lab/2024_Arevalo_NeurIPS_MotiVE, and the DGIdb, DrugRep, Hetionet, OpenBioLink and PharMeBINet source databases. Cite the corresponding papers. Source-specific terms apply; https://zenodo.org/records/18197517 describes the mixed-source annotation archive.
- Toxicity labels: TOXRIC, https://toxric.bioinforai.tech/; Wu et al., https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkac1074. TOXRIC and underlying assay terms apply. The software license does not grant rights to these labels.
- PRISM: Broad PRISM Repurposing 19Q4, https://depmap.org/repurposing/; Corsello et al., Nature Cancer (2020). DepMap-generated data use CC BY 4.0.
- Structural-alert rules: RDKit, ChEMBL, Toxtree and the literature sources identified in the alert metadata retain their respective terms.
- Molecular and protein representations: MoLFormer, ChemBERTa, NYAN and ESM2 retain their source terms. Public comparator checkpoints are projection/profile networks trained for this study.
